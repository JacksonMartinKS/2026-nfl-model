The arrows compare it with the same number of seasons and the same random draws without your picks, so they show the effect of the picks rather than noise. Picking a future winner also counts as evidence about how good the two teams are: seasons where that result was more likely count for more, the same way the rooting guide works, so the more games you pick, the fewer seasons effectively count.     How the forecast works

    Every game is decided by an Elo rating model. Ratings carry over from last season with partial reversion to the mean, update after each result based on margin of victory, and adjust each game for home field, rest, travel distance, and the starting quarterback.

    After each week, the rest of the schedule is simulated many times with random variation in every team's true strength. Each percentage is the share of simulated seasons where that outcome happened, and projected wins include games already played.
